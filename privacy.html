Tenzy Privacy Policy

Short version: what you do in Tenzy stays on your phone. There is no account and no server.

Stays on your phone

  • Photos, videos, PDFs and files you open in a tool, and the results — until you save or share them yourself
  • Scans, extracted text, receipts, warranties and the notes in your Vault
  • Voice recordings and transcripts you make in Voice to Notes or Rest, only when you tap record
  • Screenshots Memory indexes (the index is built and kept on your phone)
  • Your settings, theme and sticky notes
Stored in the app's private storage. Deleting the app deletes all of it. We can't see it.

Never collected

  • Your name, email, or any account — there isn't one
  • Analytics, tracking of what you do inside the app, or crash reports
  • Your contacts, location, photos or recordings — they are used on your phone to do the job you asked for and are never sent to us

Permissions, and why

  • Camera: Scan, Studio, Measure, Pro Camera, Identify, Translate, See & Read, Color Vision
  • Microphone: Audio Lab, Voice to Notes, Translate voice mode, Rest. Levels are measured on your phone; recordings are saved only when you start them
  • Speech recognition: transcribes your voice using Apple's speech recognition. On supported iPhones this runs on the device; otherwise iOS may send the audio to Apple under Apple's privacy policy
  • Photos: tools you pick photos in (Studio, Privacy Scrubber, Cleaner, Memory). iOS asks you to confirm every deletion
  • Contacts: the Contacts tool finds duplicates, merges them and exports a backup file, only when you open it
  • Location (while in use): compass true north, altitude and the GPS Dashboard. It stays on your phone
  • Motion: level, barometer and metal-detector readings in Measure
  • Face ID: unlocks the Vault. Tenzy never sees your face data
  • Android only: Draw over other apps (floating buttons) and Notification access (Notification Log). Tenzy uses no Accessibility service
Each permission is asked for by the tool that needs it. You can change it any time in your phone's Settings.

Network use

  • See & Read downloads a web page only when you paste its link, to read it to you
  • Android: the speed test downloads a test file from Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com) only when you tap start, so Cloudflare sees your IP address like any website you visit. Device scans stay on your own Wi-Fi
  • Ads (free version) and purchases, described below

The one exception: ads

  • The free version shows ads through Google AdMob.
  • AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and basic device information to pick and count ads, under Google's privacy policy.
  • iOS: personalised ads are shown only if you allow tracking when asked; change it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Android: reset or delete the ID in Settings › Google › Ads.
  • Tenzy Pro removes the ads and the ad code doesn't run.

Payments

Tenzy Pro is $19.99 a year with a 3-day free trial, auto-renewing, billed by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card. Cancel any time in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.

On-device AI

Text Studio, Memory, Message Shield and the other AI tools use the model built into your phone where supported. Nothing you type or scan is sent to us or to an AI service.

Children

Tenzy isn't for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
