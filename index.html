Tenzy

30 small tools in one app: the stuff your phone makes you dig for, or download five apps to do.

What it does

  • Shrink a photo, PDF or video under an exact size limit
  • Scan documents, read text, sign and fill PDFs
  • Convert HEIC and MOV to JPG and MP4 in batch, make GIFs
  • Strip location and metadata, redact faces and plates
  • Flashlight, AR tape measure, sound meter, tuner, white noise
  • Translate, magnify and read aloud, find duplicate contacts, a Face ID vault

Free and Pro

Seven everyday tools are free: Flashlight, QR & Wi-Fi, See & Read, Measure, Cleaner, Translate and the Face ID Vault. The rest are Tenzy Pro: $19.99 a year with a 3-day free trial, and Pro turns the ads off. Most of it runs on your phone, with no account and no sign-up.
